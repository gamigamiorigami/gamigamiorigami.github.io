Privacy Policy

GamiGami Origami (がみがみ折り紙) for iPhone, iPad and Android · Effective November 20, 2026

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no game server. Your progress stays on your phone.
  • The age and birth month you give Gami-sensei never leave your phone. They pick which ads you may see and when your birthday present arrives.
  • Before giving a birthday or festival present, the game checks today's date online. Nothing about you is sent.
  • To make the game better, it sends anonymous play stats (like how far players get and which folds are tricky). They carry no name, no ID, no IP address and no location, so they can't be traced back to you. You can turn them off in ⚙️ Settings.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob: videos you choose to watch for a reward, and now and then a short "word from our sponsor" between folds. Players under 13, or anyone who'd rather not say their age, only get child-directed ads that aren't personalized.
  • You can buy "Remove ads" to turn off the sponsor breaks. Apple or Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details or your store account.
  • We don't sell your information. We don't even have any that identifies you.

Who we are

GamiGami Origami is made by Tom Oliphant, an independent developer ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy or your information: gamigamiorigamigame@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The game saves everything in the app's own storage on your device: your twin and outfit, your folds and scores, papers, Gamibucks, map progress, settings, and the answer to Gami-sensei's student card (the birth year worked out from your age and birth month, or that you'd rather not say). None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, except the anonymous counts described under "Anonymous play stats" below. Deleting the app, or ⚙️ Settings › 🧰 Advanced › Reset all progress, erases it.

The student card (your age)

On first launch Gami-sensei asks your age and birth month. You can choose "I'd rather not say". The game keeps the birth year it works out from them, so the ads grow up with you. We use the answer, on your phone only, for two things:

  • Which ads you may see. Under 13, or no answer: ads are marked as directed to children (as the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act requires), are not personalized, and are limited to content suitable for general audiences. 13 to 15: ads are not personalized. 16 and over: normal ads, rated for parental guidance at most, personalized only if you agree.
  • A birthday present in the game, in your birth month.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows two kinds of video ads, both provided by Google AdMob. Rewarded ads (for example "Double it!", extra Gamibucks in the Print Shop, or an extra hint) only play when you tap to watch one. Sponsor breaks are short ads shown now and then when you leave a finished fold's results (at most one every few folds and several minutes apart, and none for new players); buying "Remove ads" turns them off. To show and measure ads, Google's software in the app may collect information such as your device type and settings, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with ads.

  • Players under 13 or of unknown age: requests are tagged as child-directed. Google does not use the advertising ID or show personalized ads for them.
  • Players 13 to 15: requests are tagged as under the age of consent, so ads are not personalized.
  • Players 16 and over: Google may use your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA if you allow tracking) to personalize ads, where you have agreed. In Europe, the UK and some US states, Google's privacy message asks you first, and you can change your choice any time in ⚙️ Settings › 🔏 Privacy › Change my ad privacy choices. On iPhone, Apple asks whether to allow tracking; saying no still shows ads, just not personalized ones. You can also reset or turn off the advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases ("Remove ads")

The game has one optional in-app purchase, "Remove ads", in ⚙️ Settings › 🚫📺 Ads. It is bought through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google), which handle the payment under their own privacy policies (Apple, Google). We don't receive your name, payment details or store account. The game asks the store on your phone whether "Remove ads" was bought, so it can restore it after a reinstall; no server of ours is involved. The only thing we learn is the anonymous play stat described below. Players under 13, or of unknown age, are asked a grown-ups' question before a purchase can start.

Anonymous play stats

To learn what to improve (which folds are too hard, where players stop, whether anyone watches the credits to the end), the game sends anonymous gameplay events to PostHog, an analytics service that processes them for us on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt). PostHog only uses them to provide the service to us.

  • What is sent: what happened in the game (for example "finished the Crane with 92% and 3 stars in 2 minutes", "a fold was sent back", "watched a rewarded ad", "a sponsor break was shown", "tapped Remove ads, and whether the purchase went through", "reached the end of the credits"), plus general counts from your save (how many origami you've folded, papers owned, chapter reached, days since you first played, how many times you've played), your age group (under 13, 13–15 or 16+, never your age or birthday), the app version, the platform (iPhone or Android), a rough device type (phone, tablet), the language, and short error messages if the game crashes.
  • What is not sent: no name, no account, no advertising ID, no device ID, no player ID, nothing you type, and no photos. Each play session gets a new random number so its events can be grouped, and it's never saved, so different sessions can't be linked to each other or to you.
  • No IP address or location: PostHog is set to discard IP addresses and does not look up your location.
  • Turning it off: ⚙️ Settings › 🔏 Privacy › 📊 Share anonymous play stats. Stats not yet sent are deleted on your phone right away, and nothing more is sent.

Because the stats can't be connected to any person, we keep them only as long as they're useful for improving the game. See PostHog's privacy policy.

Checking the date

Birthday and festival presents depend on the date, so before giving one the game checks the real date instead of trusting the phone's clock. It sends a tiny request to Google's connectivity check (connectivitycheck.gstatic.com, the same address Android phones use to see whether they're online) or, if that doesn't answer, to this website, and reads only the date and time from the reply. The request carries no information about you or your game. Like any internet request, the server sees your device's IP address: Google for the connectivity check (see Google's privacy policy), and GitHub, which hosts this website (see GitHub's privacy statement). We don't receive it.

Fonts

The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your device's IP address when the fonts are downloaded. See Google Fonts privacy.

Reminders

If you turn them on, reminders (for your daily bonus, or a festival starting) are local notifications, scheduled on your phone by the game itself. No push service or server is involved. You can turn them off in ⚙️ Settings › 🔔 Reminders or in your phone's settings.

Snapshots

📸 Snapshots are made on your phone. They're only shared if you share them yourself, through your phone's share sheet.

Children

GamiGami Origami is for all ages and made to be safe for kids. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The age answer stays on the device (only the age group goes with the anonymous play stats), the play stats can't identify anyone and can be turned off in ⚙️ Settings, children only receive child-directed, non-personalized ads, and a purchase can only start after a grown-ups' question. Parents with questions can write to gamigamiorigamigame@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU / UK GDPR, or California's CCPA / CPRA), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the use of your personal information. We don't hold any personal information about you on servers, so there is nothing for us to hand over or delete: your game data is on your device, under your control. The anonymous play stats can't be traced back to you, so we can't find or remove a particular person's; you can stop them any time in ⚙️ Settings. For information Google collects through ads, use the ad privacy choices above or Google's own tools. You can always contact us with any request.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling information differently, we'll update this page and its effective date.
